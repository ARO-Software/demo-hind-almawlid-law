## About this demo

A single-page static demo site — no backend, no build step, no external JS
libraries. `index.html` + `styles.css` + `script.js` + `/assets/`.

**Art direction** is taken directly from the office's own signage: deep
aubergine purple with gold foil, and the pointed-arch (mihrab) motif from the
ornamental band on the interior plaque. The arch is used as the page's
structural device — hero crest, the arch-masked entrance photo, section
eyebrows, and the ornamental dividers.

- **Type:** Amiri (Arabic serif, display) · Tajawal (body) · Noto Kufi Arabic (utility labels)
- **Palette:** `#2E0F35` / `#4A1B52` plum · `#C9A227` gold · `#FBF8F3` ivory
- **Sections:** hero · نبذة · مجالات العمل · من المكتب · مواعيد العمل · الموقع · طلب استشارة
- **Primary CTA:** consultation request via WhatsApp

## Content policy

Every fact on the page is taken from the office's own signage or its verified
Google listing: name, phone, email, Instagram handle, office/building number,
address, working hours, and the 4.8 Maps rating. **No** years of experience,
case counts, client numbers, success rates, testimonials, or awards are stated
anywhere — practice areas describe the service only, never an outcome.
